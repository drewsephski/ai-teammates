## Browser icon prompt

Use case: logo-brand
Asset type: Krafty browser favicon and app icon, one finished square logo.
Primary request: Design a new engaging, premium, distinctive bot brand mark for Krafty, a product of AI teammates with personality, memory, and their own computers. The existing identity is neutral black and ivory, friendly rounded bot characters.
Scene/backdrop: solid near-black charcoal square canvas, edge to edge, no mockup surround.
Subject: a single large warm-ivory organic bot face emblem, a memorable rounded four-lobed silhouette with a subtly asymmetric upward sweep suggesting energy and character. Two bold charcoal pill-shaped eyes cut into the face, slightly angled together to give an alert and friendly expression. Make the silhouette ownable and beautifully balanced, closer to a designed symbol than a cartoon.
Style/medium: exceptionally refined flat brand design, crisp antialiased edges, restrained geometric-organic form, strong negative space. Only charcoal and warm ivory.
Composition/framing: centered emblem fills approximately 78 percent of square, ample even safety margin, instantly legible as a browser favicon at 16 and 32 pixels.
Text: none.
Constraints: one icon only, front-on flat art, no letters, no words, no mouth, no antenna, no tiny details, no decorative stars, no gradients, no 3D, no shadows, no border, no watermark, no multiple alternatives. Output square.

## Issues
1. MEDIUM (honesty/trust): Homepage demo uses invented people and events presented as a live-looking workspace: "Hi Drew", "Priya's invoice question", "Leo", leo@harborcoffee.example, drew@kraftybot.app, "Asked the dentist...", timestamps "Today 1:08 PM", and approval badges in the sidebar. The only disclosure is a tiny, low-contrast gray "Example" label (~11px) in the chat header (visible in home-desktop.png, home-mobile.png) plus a "Reset example" control. Mobile shows the label but it is easy to miss. Fix: label the frame prominently ("Example conversation, not real data") near the demo, not just in the header; avoid the real-sounding name "Drew" or use a neutral persona.
2. MEDIUM (bots/SEO): The demo is server-rendered, so all fictional names/emails/approvals are in the indexable HTML (~13k chars of text; 1.07 MB HTML document). Crawlers and LLMs may read them as real claims. Fix: add "Example" wording to the demo's text content (aria-label / visually present), consider data-nosnippet on the demo, and cut the HTML weight (inlined SVG paths in the H1 mark and the demo account for much of it).
3. LOW (legibility): Hero subhead and card body text are mid-gray on near-black (approx #8a8a8a on #0a0a0a, roughly 5:1, passes AA but looks dim); the "Example" label, breadcrumbs, "Updated October 26, 2026" and desktop sidebar timestamps are smaller and fainter (likely under 4.5:1). Fix: lift secondary text lightness and set a 12px+ minimum.
4. LOW (layout): H1 contains an inline SVG mascot plus "Krafty Bot", which wraps to a second line; the H1 accessible/text content reads "AI that does the work Krafty Bot", fine. On desktop the hero is centred while use-case pages are left-aligned in a ~720px column leaving the right ~35% empty (sales-outbound-desktop.png): acceptable but weak use of space.
5. LOW (mobile nav): Mobile shows only logo, "Open Krafty" and a hamburger; the desktop nav (Features, Use cases, How it works, Pricing) is hidden behind the menu. Not verified opened. Primary CTA "Open Krafty" says sign-in-ish while hero says "Try Krafty free": two differently worded CTAs for the same destination may confuse.
6. INFO: Tap targets look adequate: mobile CTAs ~44px tall, hamburger ~52px, no overlapping elements. Base text ~16px+ on mobile. No horizontal scroll visible in any mobile screenshot (all capture exactly 750px wide, 2x of 375) but scrollWidth was not measured.
7. INFO: Sticky nav: not measurable from static capture; header appears to be static top bar in screenshots. Layout shift: no visible shift evidence; CLS not measured (a post-hydration demo with animated typing may shift).
8. INFO: Content behind JS: none of concern. H1, 9 H2s (What is Krafty Bot, Pricing, FAQs...), pricing and FAQ text are present in raw curl HTML (not JS-only). 6 inline `opacity:0` elements are animation initial states (SVG mascot shapes), not hidden copy.

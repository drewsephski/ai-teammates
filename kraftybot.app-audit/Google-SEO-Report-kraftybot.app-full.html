1. Executive Summary

This report presents a comprehensive SEO audit of kraftybot.app for a SaaS site, generated on October 27, 2026. Findings combine technical, content, schema, performance, visual, and search-readiness evidence as available.

69/100
SEO Health Score
Critical Issues Found:
  1. No off-site footprint: not in Common Crawl, no listings, mentions or sameAs profiles
  2. Thin, templated pages: nine use-case pages at 388-435 words from one skeleton; three topic pages at 370-565 words
  3. Page-type mismatch: /manus-alternatives targets a list query but names no alternatives; topic pages are product pages where guides rank
  4. Homepage weight: 1.07 MB raw HTML (95 inline SVGs), ~822 KB gzip JS, Clerk on every marketing page
  5. No proof of experience: no about page, screenshots, video, case studies or named author
Quick Wins:
  1. 301 www.kraftybot.app to the apex (serves a 200 duplicate today)
  2. Add /download to the sitemap, trim four long meta descriptions, extend the homepage footer to the 11 unlinked pages
  3. Add sameAs, seller and ContactPage to the schema; replace the personal Gmail contact with a role address
  4. Add the four topic pages to llms-full.txt and give llms.txt links their own descriptions
  5. Verify Bing Webmaster Tools, enable IndexNow, set a PageSpeed Insights API key for the next audit

2. Audit Categories

2.1 Technical SEO

Score: 86/100

What Works

  • All 20 sitemap URLs return 200, self-canonical, no robots meta or X-Robots-Tag
  • Server-rendered, raw and rendered text match, 0 console errors
  • http to https single 301; trailing slash 308; true 404 with noindex
  • HSTS, nosniff, X-Frame-Options DENY, frame-ancestors none, Referrer-Policy, nonce CSP
  • Unique titles and descriptions on every page

Findings

Homepage HTML is 1.07 MB raw High

https://kraftybot.app/ is 1,068,730 bytes raw (95 KB gzip); 95 inline SVGs account for 862 KB; inner pages are 38-68 KB.

Recommendation: Move SVGs to static files or a sprite; lazy-render below-fold sections.

No www redirect Medium

https://www.kraftybot.app/ returns 200 with a canonical to the apex.

Recommendation: 301 www to apex.

Marketing pages are uncacheable Medium

cache-control: private, no-cache, no-store on public pages because of the per-request CSP nonce.

Recommendation: Use script hashes on marketing routes or inject the nonce at the edge so the CDN can cache.

Loose CSP Medium

connect-src, frame-src, img-src and media-src include bare https:; script-src carries unsafe-inline.

Recommendation: List explicit origins only.

/download missing from sitemap Medium

/download is linked, returns 200, self-canonical and indexable but is not in /sitemap.xml.

Recommendation: Add it in app/sitemap.ts or noindex it on purpose.

HSTS without includeSubDomains or preload Low

max-age=31536000 only.

Recommendation: Add includeSubDomains; preload once all subdomains are HTTPS.

No Permissions-Policy header Low

Header absent.

Recommendation: Add a restrictive policy.

Long meta descriptions on four topic pages Low

/manus-alternatives 195, /autonomous-ai-agent 186, /ai-agents-for-business 173, /ai-employees 163 characters.

Recommendation: Trim to about 155.

404 page reuses homepage description Low

Also has two duplicate robots noindex tags.

Recommendation: Own description, single robots tag.

OG image served with max-age=0 Low

/brand/krafty-social-v1.jpg is versioned by filename.

Recommendation: Long max-age, immutable.

Sitemap lastmod equals deploy date Low

16 of 20 lastmod values are 2026-10-26; priority is set and ignored by Google.

Recommendation: Per-page content dates; drop priority.

2.2 Content Quality

Score: 60/100

What Works

  • Accurate, plain copy with no false claims; Flesch 60-81
  • Prices and limits stated openly and consistent everywhere
  • Clear definition sentence and llms.txt (AI citation readiness 74)
  • /how-it-works is a strong 772-word page

Findings

Nine use-case pages are thin and templated High

388-435 words each from one heading skeleton with five identical sentences; 45-51% unique against siblings, 40-45% once the hub (which repeats every starter prompt) is counted.

Recommendation: Add a sample output, first-run walk-through and role-specific questions per page; 700+ unique words; cut hub prompts to teasers.

Little proof of experience or authority High

E-E-A-T: Experience 38, Expertise 60, Authoritativeness 30, Trust 70. No about page, author, case study, screenshot or video.

Recommendation: Add /about, 2-3 case studies with real outputs, real screenshots, a demo video.

Topic pages restate the same features Medium

/ai-agents-for-business (565 words), /ai-employees (416), /autonomous-ai-agent (370) all repeat memory, apps, cloud computer, approvals with keyword-forced H2s.

Recommendation: One distinct angle per page; link the feature list to /how-it-works.

/manus-alternatives lists no alternatives Medium

419 words; says it does not publish comparisons; every result for the query is a list or directory.

Recommendation: Owner decision: retarget to a vs page, build a cited alternatives guide without scores, or redirect.

Trust gaps Medium

Support is a personal gmail.com address; no legal entity, address or security page.

Recommendation: Role address on the domain; data and security page; entity in terms.

No blog or guides section Info

No informational content beyond the topic pages.

Recommendation: Start with the five cluster-plan pages.

2.3 On-Page SEO

Score: 72/100

What Works

  • Unique titles; one H1 per page matching its query
  • Correct canonicals; no templated metadata detected
  • Inner pages link each other well; breadcrumbs present

Findings

Homepage footer omits 11 public pages Medium

The homepage uses a shorter footer that omits /autonomous-ai-agent, /manus-alternatives and all nine use-case pages.

Recommendation: Reuse the inner-page footer or link each homepage use-case tab to its page.

Several pages compete for 'AI agent' Medium

Pillar H1 targets the enterprise-leaning 'AI agents for business'; three pages chase the head term.

Recommendation: Pillar takes 'AI agents for small business'; /ai-employees the role framing; /autonomous-ai-agent the definition.

Page-type mismatch on topic and use-case queries High

Guides, definitions and lists rank for every target query; kraftybot.app serves 244-565 word product pages. SXO gap scores 37-45/100.

Recommendation: Add a guide layer: question H2s, direct answers, named competitors, sample output, FAQs; retarget support triage to 'AI support ticket triage'.

Short titles and long descriptions on topic pages Low

Titles 27-36 characters; descriptions 163-195.

Recommendation: About 55 and 155 characters.

Homepage H1 extracts without a separator Low

Reads 'AI that does the workKrafty Bot' in extracted text.

Recommendation: Add a space or aria-label.

Card and page claims differ Low

Sales Outbound card claims LinkedIn drafts and intent scoring the page does not mention.

Recommendation: Align card and page.

Findings

No CrUX key; PageSpeed Insights rate-limited keyless calls; Lighthouse not installed. Score is provisional from lab weight signals.

Homepage JS about 822 KB gzip across 33 chunks Medium

Recommendation: Dynamic-import the chat demo and below-fold sections; expected saving 300-500 KB gzip.

Clerk loads on every marketing page Medium

Recommendation: Load only on app and auth routes.

Homepage DOM is 2,470 elements with 95 inline SVGs Medium

Recommendation: SVG sprite and lazy render; expected 50-70% raw HTML reduction.

Four render-blocking stylesheets Low

Main sheet 40 KB gzip / 260 KB raw.

Recommendation: Purge unused CSS; split marketing CSS.

HTML served gzip, not brotli Low

Content-Encoding: gzip.

Score: 66/100

What Works

Findings

No third-party footprint High

Zero results on Wikipedia, Hacker News, GitHub, YouTube; Product Hunt 404; not in Common Crawl.

Recommendation: Launch on Product Hunt and Show HN; directory and comparison listings; LinkedIn page; YouTube demo; then sameAs.

/manus-alternatives names no alternatives High

Will not be picked for list-style queries by any AI engine.

Recommendation: Dated list of real alternatives with a criteria table, or retarget the page.

llms-full.txt omits the topic pages Medium

'Manus', 'autonomous' and 'AI employee' never appear; llms.txt descriptions reuse meta text.

Recommendation: Fix in lib/marketing/llms.ts and content.ts.

Topic-page H2s are labels, not questions Medium

/ai-employees and /ai-agents-for-business never define their own term.

Recommendation: Question H2s with a 40-60 word direct answer under each.

Homepage demo and mock form expose fictional data Medium

Fictional names, emails and timestamps in indexable HTML with an 11 px 'Example' label; the email-draft mock has about 12 unlabeled inputs.

Recommendation: Prominent example label in the demo text; neutral persona; aria-hidden or inert on the mock; aria-label on the demo form; consider data-nosnippet.

Bing indexing unverified Low

ChatGPT search and Copilot draw on Bing.

Recommendation: Bing Webmaster Tools and IndexNow.

Draft agent standards absent Low

No Content-Signal, Markdown delivery, WebMCP or /.well-known discovery files. Lighthouse agentic fraction not measured (PSI 429).

3. Action Plan

Phase 1: Quick wins Week 1

  • 301 www to apex
  • Add /download to sitemap; trim four long descriptions; extend homepage footer to the 11 unlinked pages
  • Schema: seller, ContactPage on /support, WebPage on /privacy, role email in contactPoint, real dateModified
  • Add topic pages to llms-full.txt; own descriptions in llms.txt
  • Bing Webmaster Tools + IndexNow; PageSpeed Insights API key; Search Console
  • Decide the /manus-alternatives direction; start the homepage weight work

Phase 2: High-impact improvements Weeks 2-3

  • Deepen the nine use-case pages (700+ unique words, sample output, walk-through, role questions); cut hub prompts to teasers
  • Give each topic page one angle with question H2s and direct answers; retarget support triage
  • Cut homepage weight: SVG sprite, dynamic imports, Clerk off marketing routes, CSS purge
  • Label the demo chat as an example in its own text; aria-hidden the mock form
  • Add /about, real screenshots, demo video, 2-3 case studies; show the $20 price and a security page on every page

Phase 3: Content & authority Month 2

  • Launch week: Product Hunt, Show HN, AlternativeTo, G2, Capterra, AI directories, LinkedIn, YouTube; then sameAs
  • New pages: /ai-agent-human-approval, /integrations/gmail, /ai-agent-cloud-computer, /integrations/google-sheets, /ai-agent-vs-chatbot
  • Cacheable marketing pages (hash-based CSP or edge nonce); explicit CSP origins

Phase 4: Monitoring & iteration Ongoing

  • Capture a drift baseline now; re-audit after launch week, then quarterly
  • Watch Search Console and Bing impressions for the 'ai agent for small business', 'human approval' and 'gmail' families
  • Monthly count of third-party pages mentioning Krafty Bot (target 10 within 60 days of launch)
  • PageSpeed Insights monthly once keyed; CrUX once traffic qualifies
  • Low backlog: HSTS preload, Permissions-Policy, OG image cache, brotli, sitemap lastmod, contrast, H1 separator
